# Step 7: Music Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or
> superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A workout plays the program's playlist on a Music Assistant speaker, chosen on the details step. The music
pauses, resumes and fades with the workout, and there's a music player in the timer's header. A dev-only fake Music
Assistant stands in for the real one.

**Architecture:** The add-on freezes the music into the session plan and lists speakers and playlists for Claude, read
from HA. The integration's music controller follows the session view and calls HA's services. The cards show a
speaker dropdown and a header player.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.14 (FastAPI, FastMCP, pydantic, httpx, respx) in the add-on; a Home Assistant custom
integration (pytest-homeassistant-custom-component); Lit 3 and TypeScript (Vitest) in the cards.

**Spec:** [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-music-design.md`](../specs/2026-10-24-music-design.md)

## Global Constraints

- **Two PRs** (spec §2):
  - PR 1, `feat: music in the add-on`, on branch `feat/music-addon`: Tasks 1–4.
  - PR 2, `feat: music on the tablet`, on branch `feat/music-tablet` off `main` after PR 1 merges: Tasks 5–10.
- **Hard rule 2:** no data from Thomas's HA anywhere. The fake's players are `media_player.fake_kitchen`,
  `media_player.fake_living_room` and `media_player.fake_garage`.
- **Hard rule 5:** tests go through public interfaces: REST and MCP responses, services, ws commands, entity states,
  rendered text and roles. A frozen clock for anything time-based.
- **Hard rule 6:** every UI text comes from a language file, in English and Danish.
- **Hard rule 7:** never read anything under `config/` or `local/`, and never bypass a hook.
- **`api_version` stays 1.**
- **The speaker pattern** everywhere is `^media_player\.[a-z0-9_]+$`, the one on `Music.speaker` (`schemas.py`).
- **The fade:** 5 s, 10 steps, paced every 0.5 s from the fade's start.
- **Commits:** `git commit -F <file>`, with the co-author trailer the dispatch gives.

## Review Focus

1. **A speaker unavailable at the start, then available again.** Expect the music to start then, while the workout
   runs (spec §5.1). Pinned by Task 7's test `test_failed_start_retried_when_speaker_returns`.
2. **An HA restart in the middle of a workout.** Expect no restart of the playlist, while Pause, Resume and Slut still
   act on the speaker. Pinned by Task 7's `test_adopted_session_pauses_but_never_starts`.
3. **The integration against an older add-on.** Expect Start without `speaker`, no music row and no player. Pinned by
   Task 5's `test_no_music_without_music_supported`.
4. **Slut while paused.** Expect no fade and no pause call. Pinned by Task 7's `test_no_fade_when_not_playing`.
5. **A long speaker name or playlist name in portrait.** Expect ellipsis, with the pills and Slut kept. Checked in the
   controller's Chrome check (Task 10), since tests can't check layout.
